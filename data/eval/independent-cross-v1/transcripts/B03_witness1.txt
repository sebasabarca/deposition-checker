 1                     DISTRICT COURT, PELLHAM COUNTY
 2        TOMAS FERREIRA, Plaintiff, v. BRIGHTLINE BUILDERS, INC.,
 3   and TALLIS SCAFFOLD CO., Defendants.  Civil Action No. 26-CV-0389
 4            DEPOSITION OF OWEN CASTELLANO - August 19, 2026
 5     OWEN CASTELLANO, called as a witness, being first duly sworn,
 6                         testified as follows:
 7                              EXAMINATION
 8   BY MR. DIAZ:
 9   MR. DIAZ:  Mr. Castellano, what is your job with Brightline
10   Builders?
11   THE WITNESS:  I'm a, I guess you'd call it a site superintendent.
12   That's the title. I've been with Brightline, oh, about nine years,
13   maybe a little more, superintendent for the last four of those.
14   MR. DIAZ:  In March of 2026, what project were you the
15   superintendent on?
16   THE WITNESS:  The mid-rise at 400 Quarry Street. Six stories,
17   mixed use, retail on the bottom, apartments up top.
18   MR. DIAZ:  Who supplied the scaffolding on that project?
19   THE WITNESS:  Tallis. Tallis Scaffold Company. They erected it,
20   they inspected it, they tagged it. That was their scope, as I
21   understood it.
22   MR. DIAZ:  Who from Tallis was responsible for inspecting it?
23   THE WITNESS:  Rosalind Achterberg, mostly. She was their competent
24   person on the job. She was out there most mornings, I think.
25   MR. DIAZ:  How was the scaffold divided?

                                - 4 -
 1   THE WITNESS:  Into bays. On the north elevation there were bays A
 2   through F, I believe, with A at the west end.
 3   MR. DIAZ:  I want to focus on Bay C on the morning of March 12,
 4   2026. Did you walk the north elevation that morning?
 5   THE WITNESS:  I did. I walk the whole site first thing, I try to,
 6   every day. That day I was on the north side at seven, right around
 7   seven.
 8   MR. DIAZ:  What color was the inspection tag on Bay C when you
 9   walked it at seven?
10   MS. LINDQVIST:  Objection, form.
11   MR. DIAZ:  You can answer.
12   THE WITNESS:  It was green. The tag on Bay C was green at seven. I
13   remember because I, I looked at each tag going down the line, and
14   they were all green that morning, as far as I, yes, all green.
15   MR. DIAZ:  Did you look at the base of the Bay C scaffold that
16   morning?
17   THE WITNESS:  I did, I believe I did, yes. I usually look at the
18   legs.
19   MR. DIAZ:  What about the northeast leg of Bay C?
20   THE WITNESS:  The northeast leg, that was sitting, it had a
21   two-by-ten pad under it. I'm pretty sure, I looked right at it
22   when I was walking past, at seven. I'd say I'm confident on that.
23   MR. DIAZ:  What was the ground like under that part of the
24   scaffold?
25   THE WITNESS:  Fill. Compacted fill, more or less. It had rained a

                                - 5 -
 1   couple of days earlier, so it was, it was soft in spots, I'd say.
 2   MR. DIAZ:  What time did the collapse happen?
 3   THE WITNESS:  Around ten-forty. Maybe ten forty-five. I was in the
 4   trailer when I heard it.
 5   MR. DIAZ:  How many people were on the Bay C platform when it came
 6   down?
 7   THE WITNESS:  Three. There were three masons on the Bay C platform
 8   when it went. Tomas, and two others from the masonry sub, I
 9   believe.
10   MR. DIAZ:  Who directed them to work from Bay C that morning?
11   THE WITNESS:  The masonry foreman, I assume. I didn't direct them.
12   I, I don't remember anybody asking me about it.
13   MR. DIAZ:  Did you have the authority to stop work on a scaffold
14   you thought was unsafe?
15   THE WITNESS:  Yes, I had the authority to shut down any scaffold
16   on that site. Anybody does, really, but as superintendent, yes,
17   for sure.
18   MR. DIAZ:  Did you stop work on Bay C at any point that morning?
19   THE WITNESS:  No. I didn't see any reason to, at the time.
20   MR. DIAZ:  What was the weather like?
21   THE WITNESS:  Windy. It was gusting pretty hard, I'd guess thirty,
22   thirty-five. Miles an hour. Up on the deck especially.
23   MR. DIAZ:  When were you up on the deck?
24   THE WITNESS:  At some point that morning. I, I'd have to think
25   about the exact time. Before the collapse, I know that.

                                - 6 -
 1   MR. DIAZ:  Was wind speed measured anywhere on site?
 2   THE WITNESS:  I think Tallis had a gauge in their trailer. I
 3   didn't look at it that day.
 4       (Castellano Exhibit 12 was marked for identification.)
 5   MR. DIAZ:  Exhibit 12 is a page from your daily superintendent's
 6   report for March 12, 2026. Is that your report?
 7   THE WITNESS:  Yeah, that's mine. That's my signature at the
 8   bottom.
 9   MR. DIAZ:  Under weather, it says "windy, cool." Did you write
10   that?
11   THE WITNESS:  I did. I don't usually put numbers in there. Just a
12   general description.
13   MR. DIAZ:  Does the report mention the tag color on any scaffold?
14   THE WITNESS:  No. We don't record tags on that report. That's
15   Tallis's paperwork.
16   MR. DIAZ:  After the collapse, what did you do?
17   THE WITNESS:  I ran out, I had my assistant call 911, and I went
18   to Tomas. He was on the ground, conscious, he was talking. The
19   other two had grabbed onto the frame on the way down, more or
20   less, and they were hanging on near the second-floor level.
21   MR. DIAZ:  Did you talk with Ms. Achterberg that day?
22   THE WITNESS:  Later. She came back to the site after it happened.
23   Around noon, I would say.
24   MR. DIAZ:  What did she say?
25   MS. LINDQVIST:  Objection, hearsay.

                                - 7 -
 1   MR. DIAZ:  She's a co-defendant's employee. Go ahead.
 2   THE WITNESS:  She was upset. She asked who had been up on it. I
 3   told her. That's about all I remember.
 4   MR. DIAZ:  Has Brightline changed anything since the collapse?
 5   MS. LINDQVIST:  Objection. Subsequent remedial measures.
 6   THE WITNESS:  We do a joint walk with the scaffold company every
 7   morning now. Both of us together, and we sign a sheet.
 8   MR. DIAZ:  I'll pass the witness.
 9   MS. LINDQVIST:  No questions.
10       (Deposition concluded at 4:05 p.m.)
