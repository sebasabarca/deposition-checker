 2        LYLE PRUETT, Plaintiff, v. CASTELLANE FREIGHT LINES, INC.,

 4             DEPOSITION OF DANA OKAFOR, taken March 10, 2026
 5     DANA OKAFOR, having been first duly sworn, testified as follows:
 6                               EXAMINATION
 7    BY MS. HADLEY:
 8    Q.   Good morning. Would you state your full name for the record,
 9    please?
10    A.   Dana Marie Okafor.
11    Q.   Ms. Okafor, have you ever had your deposition taken before?
12    A.   No. This is the first time.
13    Q.   You understand you're under oath, the same as if you were in
14    a courtroom?
15    A.   I do.
16    Q.   If you don't understand a question, tell me and I'll rephrase
17    it. Fair?
18    A.   Fair.
19    Q.   What do you do for a living?
20    A.   I'm a pharmacist. I work at the hospital outpatient pharmacy
21    in Millbrook, weekdays, eight to four-thirty.
22    Q.   I want to direct your attention to October 14, 2025. Were you
23    driving on State Route 9 that afternoon?
24    A.   Yes. I was northbound, driving home from work.
25    Q.   What were you driving?

                                                                     5
 1    A.   A silver 2019 sedan. My own car.
 2    Q.   About what time did you leave work?
 3    A.   Four thirty-five. I clocked out at four thirty-two and walked
 4    straight to the garage.
 5    Q.   How many lanes does Route 9 have northbound in that area?
 6    A.   Three, near the Millbrook interchange. Left, center and
 7    right.
 8    Q.   Which lane were you in?
 9    A.   The center lane. I'd been in the center lane since I got on
10    at Harbor Road.
11    Q.   Were there other vehicles around you as you approached mile
12    marker 112?
13    A.   Yes. The pickup was in the left lane, a little ahead of me.
14    And there was a tractor-trailer in the right lane, slightly behind
15    me and to my right.
16    Q.   How fast were you going?
17    A.   Sixty-five. The limit there is sixty-five and I had the
18    cruise control set.
19    Q.   And the truck?
20    A.   I'd say the truck was over seventy. When I first noticed it
21    in my mirror it was closing on me, and I was doing sixty-five, so
22    it had to be going faster than that.
23    Q.   Where was the truck when you first noticed it?
24    A.   Behind me in the right lane, maybe a quarter mile back. That
25    was about a mile before the interchange.

                                                                     6
 1    Q.   Did there come a time when the truck moved out of the right
 2    lane?
 3    A.   Yes. Just past the 112 marker it came left into my lane.
 4    Q.   At the moment the truck began moving into the center lane,
 5    was any turn signal on the truck activated?
 6         MR. CORBIN:  Objection, foundation.
 7         MS. HADLEY:  You can answer.
 8    A.   I was looking right at it. There was no signal on the cab or
 9    the trailer when it came over. No blinker at all.
10    Q.   How do you know?
11    A.   Because I was looking at the front corner of the cab. It was
12    right beside my passenger window. If the blinker had been going I
13    would have seen the amber light.
14    Q.   What happened next?
15    A.   The front corner of the trailer hit my passenger side, behind
16    the door. My car turned left and slid into the left lane, and I
17    hit the pickup.
18    Q.   What part of your car hit the pickup?
19    A.   My front bumper struck its rear quarter panel, on the
20    passenger side.
21    Q.   Was it raining at the time of the collision?
22    A.   No. The pavement was dry when the truck hit me. It didn't
23    start to rain until after the ambulance left, maybe forty minutes
24    later.
25    Q.   Were your windshield wipers on at the time?

                                                                     7
 1    A.   No. They hadn't been on all day.
 2        (Okafor Exhibit 3 was marked for identification.)
 3    Q.   Ms. Okafor, I've handed you what's been marked Exhibit 3. Do
 4    you recognize it?
 5    A.   Yes. It's a photograph of my car on the shoulder after the
 6    crash. I took it with my phone.
 7    Q.   When did you take it?
 8    A.   At 5:02 p.m. The time is in the phone's photo information. I
 9    checked it last week.
10    Q.   Does the photograph fairly and accurately show your car as it
11    was then?
12    A.   Yes.
13    Q.   After the collision, did you get out of your car?
14    A.   Not right away. I sat there for a minute. Then I got out and
15    walked to the shoulder.
16    Q.   What did you see the truck driver do?
17    A.   He got down from the cab and walked back along the trailer. I
18    lost track of him after that because I was watching the pickup.
19    Q.   Did anyone set out flares or warning devices?
20    A.   The man driving the pickup was the one who put out flares. He
21    lit three of them behind the pickup, in the left lane.
22    Q.   Did you speak with the truck driver at the scene?
23    A.   Briefly. He asked if I was all right. I said I thought so.
24    Q.   Did he say anything else?
25         MR. CORBIN:  Objection, hearsay.

                                                                     8
 1         MS. HADLEY:  It's a party admission. You can answer.
 2    A.   He said, "I didn't see you there." That's all.
 3    Q.   Were you injured?
 4    A.   A bruised left shoulder and a sore neck. I saw my own doctor
 5    two days later. I didn't miss any work.
 6    Q.   Who was in the pickup?
 7    A.   Two men. The driver and a passenger. The passenger was
 8    holding his arm.
 9    Q.   Did police come to the scene?
10    A.   A state trooper, yes. Trooper Wallace. I gave her a statement
11    standing next to her car.
12    Q.   Did you tell Trooper Wallace what you've told us today about
13    the turn signal?
14    A.   Yes. I told her there was no signal.
15         MS. HADLEY:  Thank you, Ms. Okafor. I'll pass the witness.
