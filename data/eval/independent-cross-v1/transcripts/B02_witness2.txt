                  SUPERIOR COURT, CARROW COUNTY
    Linden, et al. v. Saffron Table LLC, Case No. CV-2025-1182
           Deposition of Marco Tellez, December 2, 2025
      MARCO TELLEZ, having been sworn, testified as follows:
                           EXAMINATION
BY MR. OYELARAN:
Q: State your name, please.
A: Marco Antonio Tellez.
Q: Where do you work now?
A: Brasa Grill, on Fenwick Avenue.
Q: How long have you been there?
A: Since August.
Q: Before that you worked at Saffron Table?
A: Yes.
Q: From when to when?
A: January to August of this year.
Q: What was your job there?
A: Line cook.
Q: What stations did you work?
A: Grill mostly. Sometimes saute. Prep when they were short.
Q: Who was your supervisor?
A: Priya. Priya Wendell.
Q: Did you work on Thursday, June 5, 2025?
A: Yes. Eleven to close.
Q: What station were you on that Thursday?
A: Grill. The whole shift.
Q: Did you prepare the chicken salad that Thursday?
A: No. I didn't touch the chicken salad Thursday.
Q: How many batches of chicken salad were made that week?
A: One. Thursday. We only made it once a week.
Q: Do you know who made it?
A: No. I was on grill. I didn't see.
Q: Had you ever made the chicken salad at Saffron Table?
A: A couple times. In the spring.
Q: Did you work on Friday, June 6?
A: Yes. I opened.
Q: With whom?
A: Me and Priya.
Q: What time did you get there?
A: Like six twenty-five.
Q: What did you do first?
A: Pulled produce out of the walk-in. Same as always.
Q: Did you look at the walk-in thermometer that morning?
A: Yes.
Q: What time?
A: Six-thirty. When we went in.
Q: Which thermometer?
A: The dial inside the door. That's the only one in there.
Q: What did it read?
A: Fifty-one. The dial said fifty-one.
Q: Did you say anything about it?
A: I said it felt warm in there.
Q: Did you write anything in the temperature log?
A: No. Priya does the log.
Q: Let's talk about sanitizer. Did you use the sanitizer buckets?
A: Every day.
Q: Did you ever test them?
A: Sometimes. When Priya told us to.
Q: What did the strips show?
A: The strips barely changed color. Like it was water.
Q: When was that?
A: Some day that week. I don't know which day.
Q: Which bucket?
A: I don't remember. One on the line, I think.
Q: Did you tell anybody?
A: No. I dumped it and made a new one.
Q: When did you hear people got sick?
A: Friday night. Priya told the crew at close.
Q: How many people got sick?
MS. FAIRCLOTH: Objection, foundation.
A: Twenty-four. That's what Priya said Friday night.
Q: Did you hear any other number later?
A: No. I didn't ask.
Q: Were you there for the inspection?
A: Monday? No. I was off Monday.
Q: Have you seen any inspection report?
A: One.
Q: How many violations did it list?
A: The one I saw had two violations.
Q: Which report was that?
A: The one from corporate. The audit after. They had it on the
board in the office.
Q: Do you know what the two violations were?
A: Door gasket on the walk-in. And the labels.
Q: Did anyone from the county ever talk to you?
A: A lady called me. Asked what I ate that week and if I was sick.
Q: Were you sick?
A: No.
Q: Why did you leave Saffron Table?
A: Better pay. Brasa offered two dollars more an hour.
Q: Was it because of what happened in June?
MS. FAIRCLOTH: Objection, form.
A: No. Just the money.
Q: Were you ever disciplined at Saffron Table?
A: No.
Q: Did anybody talk to you about this deposition?
A: The lawyer. Ms. Faircloth. Like twenty minutes last week.
Q: Did you review any documents?
A: The temperature log. She showed me.
Q: Exhibit 7, the cooler log?
A: Yes, that one.
Q: Is your handwriting anywhere on it for June 6?
A: No.
MR. OYELARAN: Nothing further.
MS. FAIRCLOTH: No questions.
    (Deposition concluded at 2:20 p.m.)
