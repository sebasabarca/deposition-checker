 1                      DISTRICT COURT, PELLHAM COUNTY
 2              FERREIRA v. BRIGHTLINE BUILDERS, INC., et al.
 3                       Civil Action No. 26-CV-0389
 4          DEPOSITION OF ROSALIND ACHTERBERG - September 2, 2026
 5            ROSALIND ACHTERBERG, sworn, testified as follows:
 6                               EXAMINATION
 7    BY MR. DIAZ:
 8    Q.   Ms. Achterberg, by whom are you employed?
 9    A.   Tallis Scaffold Company. I'm a field supervisor and the
10    designated competent person for scaffold on the projects I'm
11    assigned to.
12    Q.   What training do you have for that role?
13    A.   An OSHA thirty-hour card, a supported-scaffold competent
14    person course through the trade association, renewed every three
15    years, and fourteen years of erecting and inspecting frame
16    scaffold.
17    Q.   Were you assigned to the 400 Quarry Street project in March
18    2026?
19    A.   Yes. Tallis erected the frame scaffold on the north and east
20    elevations. I performed the inspections.
21    Q.   Describe the tag system Tallis used on that job.
22    A.   Three colors. Green means inspected and safe for use. Yellow
23    means usable with a stated restriction. Red means do not use.
24    Every bay carries a tag at its access ladder.
25    Q.   Who is permitted to change a tag?

                                                                     6
 1    A.   Only Tallis's competent person. On that project, that was me.
 2    Q.   Directing your attention to March 11, 2026. Did you inspect
 3    Bay C on the north elevation that day?
 4    A.   Yes. At about four-fifteen in the afternoon.
 5    Q.   What did you find?
 6    A.   Settlement at the northeast leg. The base plate had dropped
 7    about three-quarters of an inch relative to the adjacent legs, and
 8    the plank was out of level.
 9    Q.   What did you do?
10    A.   I hung a red tag on Bay C at four-thirty and noted it in the
11    inspection log.
12    Q.   When did you next see Bay C?
13    A.   The next morning, March 12. I was at Bay C from about six
14    fifty-five until about seven-fifteen.
15    Q.   What color was the tag on Bay C at seven a.m. on March 12?
16         MR. VOGEL:  Objection, form.
17    A.   Red. Bay C carried a red tag at seven a.m. on the twelfth. It
18    was the tag I'd hung the day before. I checked it when I arrived
19    and again before I left.
20    Q.   Was there a mudsill under the northeast leg of Bay C on the
21    morning of March 12?
22    A.   No mudsill. No timber of any kind under that leg. The base
23    plate was bearing directly on soil. I looked at it at about seven
24    that morning, and that was the cause of the settlement.
25    Q.   Who installed the base of that scaffold?

                                                                     7
 1    A.   Tallis did, in February. The erection drawings called for a
 2    two-by-ten mudsill under every leg bearing on soil.
 3    Q.   Do you know how it came to be missing?
 4         MR. VOGEL:  Objection, calls for speculation.
 5    A.   No. I don't know.
 6    Q.   Did you take any action on the morning of March 12 regarding
 7    Bay C?
 8    A.   I confirmed the red tag was in place and requested a crew to
 9    reset the base. The crew was scheduled for that afternoon.
10    Q.   Did Mr. Castellano shut down Bay C at any time on March 12?
11    A.   Owen didn't shut down Bay C. Nobody from Brightline did.
12    There was no reason for them to. It was already tagged out.
13    Q.   When did you leave the site that morning?
14    A.   At about seven-fifteen. I had an inspection at another
15    project across town.
16    Q.   Who was on the Bay C platform when it collapsed?
17    A.   I wasn't there. As far as I knew, nobody was supposed to be
18    on Bay C that day. I wasn't told of any work planned there.
19    Q.   How did you learn of the collapse?
20    A.   Our office called me at about ten-fifty. I drove back and
21    arrived around noon.
22    Q.   Did Tallis measure wind on that site?
23    A.   Yes. We keep a handheld anemometer in the trailer, and the
24    reading goes in the daily log.
25        (Achterberg Exhibit 4 was marked for identification.)

                                                                     8
 1    Q.   Exhibit 4 is the Tallis daily scaffold log for March 12. What
 2    does it show for wind?
 3    A.   The anemometer read twelve miles an hour, gusts to fifteen.
 4    That is my entry.
 5    Q.   At what time was that reading taken?
 6    A.   In the morning. The entry doesn't have a time on it. I would
 7    normally take it when I arrive.
 8    Q.   Where was the reading taken?
 9    A.   At ground level, beside the trailer.
10    Q.   Does Tallis restrict work on scaffold above a certain wind
11    speed?
12    A.   Our policy suspends work on frame scaffold with sustained
13    wind above twenty-five miles an hour or gusts above thirty-five.
14    Q.   After the collapse, did you inspect the remaining bays?
15    A.   Yes. That afternoon I inspected bays A, B, D, E and F. They
16    remained green.
17    Q.   Have you discussed this testimony with anyone at Brightline?
18    A.   No.
19         MR. DIAZ:  That's all I have.
20         MR. VOGEL:  No questions from Tallis.
21        (Deposition concluded at 11:30 a.m.)
