 4           DEPOSITION OF SAMUEL PRENTISS - September 29, 2026
 5     SAMUEL PRENTISS, having been duly sworn, testified as follows:
 6                              EXAMINATION
 7   BY MS. OKONJO:
 8   MS. OKONJO:  Mr. Prentiss, what is your position with First
 9   Meridian Community Bank?
10   THE WITNESS:  Treasury Services Officer. I support commercial
11   clients who use our online banking and wire services.
12   MS. OKONJO:  How long have you been in that role?
13   THE WITNESS:  Six years. Before that I spent four years in deposit
14   operations at the bank.
15   MS. OKONJO:  Was Harrowgate Supply one of the clients you
16   supported in April 2026?
17   THE WITNESS:  Yes. I have supported the Harrowgate relationship
18   since 2022.
19   MS. OKONJO:  Describe how an outgoing wire is processed through
20   the portal.
21   THE WITNESS:  A user enters the wire in the client portal. If the
22   client's profile requires a second approver, the wire waits in the
23   portal for that approval. Once approved, or immediately if the
24   profile is single approval, the wire passes to our back-office
25   payment system. Wires above a threshold are held there for a

                                - 5 -
 1   callback before release.
 2   MS. OKONJO:  What approval setting did Harrowgate's profile have
 3   on April 17, 2026?
 4   THE WITNESS:  As of April 17, Harrowgate's profile was set to
 5   single approval. Dual control had been disabled in our system
 6   before that date.
 7   MS. OKONJO:  Who disabled it?
 8   MR. BRANNIGAN:  Objection, foundation.
 9   THE WITNESS:  The change was entered by bank staff in response to
10   a request. I don't have the request in front of me.
11   MS. OKONJO:  What was the wire limit for Harrowgate?
12   THE WITNESS:  The limit was five hundred thousand. That is the
13   figure in the profile as I reviewed it for this deposition.
14   MS. OKONJO:  Is that a per-wire limit or a daily limit?
15   THE WITNESS:  It is labeled the daily company limit. Users can
16   also have individual limits. I did not review the individual user
17   limits.
18   MS. OKONJO:  Had the limit ever been changed?
19   THE WITNESS:  The limits on the account were updated at some point
20   during the relationship. I can't give you dates without the change
21   history.
22   MS. OKONJO:  Let's turn to the Kessler wire. When was it entered?
23   THE WITNESS:  The portal shows it was entered at 10:31 a.m. on
24   April 17 under Jordan Mills's credentials.
25   MS. OKONJO:  What happened to it after it was entered?

                                - 6 -
 1   THE WITNESS:  Because the profile was single approval, it passed
 2   directly to our back-office system. At 10:45 the wire was sitting
 3   in pending status in our back-office queue, held for callback
 4   because it exceeded our callback threshold.
 5   MS. OKONJO:  Would the wire have been visible in the client portal
 6   at that time?
 7   THE WITNESS:  Not as pending. The client portal does not display
 8   wires that are held for callback. That status exists only in our
 9   back-office system. In the portal it would have appeared under
10   completed submissions.
11   MS. OKONJO:  Was a callback made?
12   THE WITNESS:  Yes. I placed the callback to Ms. Vukovic at 10:52
13   a.m. on April 17. I called the number on file for her, her direct
14   desk line, and she verbally confirmed the wire.
15   MS. OKONJO:  How do you know you spoke with Ms. Vukovic?
16   THE WITNESS:  The person who answered identified herself as Teresa
17   Vukovic and correctly gave the verification code associated with
18   the profile.
19   MS. OKONJO:  Is the callback recorded?
20   THE WITNESS:  It is logged in the back-office system with my user
21   ID and the time. Calls on that line were not audio recorded in
22   April.
