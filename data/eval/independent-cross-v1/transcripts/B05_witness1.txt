                  SUPERIOR COURT, DUNMORE COUNTY
      HARROWGATE SUPPLY CO. v. FIRST MERIDIAN COMMUNITY BANK
                       Case No. 26-CV-2214
         Deposition of Teresa Vukovic, September 15, 2026
TERESA VUKOVIC, having been duly sworn, was examined and testified as follows:
                           EXAMINATION
BY MR. BRANNIGAN:
Q: Ms. Vukovic, what is your position at Harrowgate Supply?
A: I'm the controller. I've been controller for, it'll be five
years in January, and before that I was the accounting manager
there for about three years, so I've been there eight years, give
or take.
Q: What are your duties as controller?
A: Pretty much everything on the accounting side. Month-end close,
payables oversight, the bank relationship, I sign off on payment
runs, I deal with the auditors. We're a small shop, there's me and
three people under me.
Q: Who are those three people?
A: Jordan Mills does payables. Alicia Fenn does receivables and
payroll. And we have a part-time staff accountant, Ben Ostrow.
Q: Who had access to the bank's online portal in April 2026?
A: Me, Jordan, and our CFO, Martin Keel. Alicia had view-only, I
think, I'd have to check that.
Q: Was the account set up to require more than one person to
approve a wire?
A: Yes. As of April 17, our account required two approvers for any
wire. Two. Always. That's how we set it up when we opened the
account, and I never asked anyone to change it, and I'm the one
who would have.
Q: What was the wire limit on the account?
A: Our wire limit was two hundred fifty thousand. That's what I
remember being told when we set it up, I think, two-fifty.
Q: Let's talk about April 17, 2026. What happened that morning?
A: Jordan got an email, what looked like an email from Kessler
Polymer, one of our resin suppliers, saying they'd changed banks
and giving new wire instructions. And there was an invoice
attached, a real-looking invoice, four hundred eighty-six thousand
dollars, which is about right for a quarter's resin.
Q: Did you see that email before the wire went out?
A: No. I didn't see it until after. Jordan forwarded it to me that
afternoon.
Q: What were you doing between ten and eleven that morning?
A: I was at my desk, mostly, working on the quarterly sales tax
filings. I had the bank portal open in a tab because I always do.
Q: Did you look at the portal during that hour?
A: Yes, around ten forty-five, I'd say, I went in to check our
balance. When I looked at the portal, there was no wire pending. I
looked at the pending tab and the balance, that's all.
Q: Did anyone from First Meridian call you on April 17?
MS. OKONJO: Objection, form.
A: No. Nobody from the bank called me that day. Not on my desk
line, not on my cell. I've gone through it, our IT pulled the desk
line log, and I have my cell bill. Nothing from the bank that day.
Q: Did you approve the Kessler wire?
A: No. I never approved it. I didn't know it existed until it was
gone.
Q: When did you learn the wire had gone out?
A: Late that afternoon, maybe four-thirty. Jordan came into my
office and said Kessler's real receivables person had called
asking about payment, and that's when, that's when my stomach
dropped.
Q: What did you do?
A: I called our relationship manager at the bank, but it was
nearly five by then, and I got voicemail. I left a message. Then I
called Martin.
Q: When did you report the wire as fraudulent to the bank?
A: I reported it to the bank the next business day. First thing. I
was on the phone with their fraud line at eight a.m.
Q: Did you get a reference number?
A: Yes. I have it in my notes, it's in the documents we produced.
    (Vukovic Exhibit 2 was marked for identification.)
Q: Exhibit 2 is the email Mr. Mills received on April 17. Is that
the email you saw that afternoon?
A: Yes. That's it. You can see the domain is off by one letter,
it's kesslerpolyrner, with an r and an n instead of the m. I
didn't catch that at first either.
Q: Has any of the money been recovered?
A: About sixty-two thousand. The rest is gone, as far as we know.
Q: Has Harrowgate changed any procedures since then?
MS. OKONJO: Objection, relevance.
A: We call vendors at a known number now before any change in bank
instructions. We should have been doing that before. I'll admit
that.
Q: Was Mr. Mills disciplined?
A: He got a written warning. He's still with us.
MR. BRANNIGAN: I have nothing further.
MS. OKONJO: Nothing from me.
    (Deposition concluded at 3:12 p.m.)
