DEPOSITION OF NADIA OKAFOR
Halvorsen Freight Co. v. Brightloom Systems, Inc.
August 20, 2025

EXAMINATION BY MR. BELLAMY
Q: Ms. Okafor, please state your name for the record.
A: Nadia Okafor.
Q: Where do you work?
A: Halvorsen Freight Company. I'm the director of information
technology.
Q: How long have you held that title?
A: Four years.
Q: Before that?
A: Systems manager at Halvorsen. Six years.
Q: Have you been deposed before?
A: Once. An employment matter. 2021.
Q: How many people are on your team?
A: Eleven. Two of them worked on RouteDesk with me.
Q: What system did Halvorsen use for dispatch before RouteDesk?
A: A homegrown system. About fifteen years old. Built on a database
we could no longer get support for.
Q: Why did Halvorsen choose Brightloom?
A: Price and timeline. They promised a November go-live.
Q: Were you involved in the RouteDesk project with Brightloom?
A: Yes. I was Halvorsen's project lead from contract signing to
termination.
Q: When was the contract signed?
A: March 2024.
Q: What was the delivery date for version 2.0?
A: November 1, 2024.
Q: How was the 2.0 release to be delivered?
A: Upload to our SFTP server, into a folder named for the release.
That's in the statement of work.
Q: Did Brightloom deliver the 2.0 release build to that server by
November 1?
A: No. There was nothing from Brightloom on our SFTP server until
November 6. I checked the server on October 31 and again on November
1. The 2.0 folder was empty both times.
Q: How did you check?
A: Logged in with the admin account and listed the folder. I also
checked the transfer log.
Q: Are you aware Brightloom says it uploaded the build on October
30?
MS. QUARRIE: Objection, form.
A: I'm aware they say that. It wasn't there.
Q: Was there an acceptance test of the 2.0 release?
A: Yes. November 12, at our office, in our staging environment.
Brightloom attended. Mr. Thorpe and two engineers.
Q: What build was tested?
A: The build uploaded November 6. Version 2.0.0.
Q: What happened during the test of the route optimization module?
A: At the acceptance test on November 12 the route optimizer crashed
every time we loaded more than fifty stops. Every time. We ran the
200-stop scenario three times. It crashed three times.
Q: Who was operating the system?
A: Our dispatcher, Leon Abara. Mr. Thorpe was next to him.
(Exhibit 21 was marked for identification.)
Q: Is Exhibit 21 the acceptance test log for November 12?
A: It's our log. Brightloom kept its own.
Q: Who prepared it?
A: I did. Same day.
Q: Let's talk about change orders. Did you sign change order 4?
A: Yes. I signed change order 4 for the mobile app on August 9.
Q: What did it cover?
A: A driver mobile app. Android only. An added $46,000.
Q: Let's turn to the CSV export feature. Did it work?
A: The CSV export never worked for us. We'd click export and get an
error.
Q: When was that?
A: September 2024. Our test environment. Version 1.9.
Q: Did you test CSV export on any later version?
A: No. We terminated before 2.0.1 was installed.
Q: How did the system perform after it went live?
A: It went live in production on December 2, on 2.0.0, with the
optimizer turned off. In December, in production, the dispatch
screen took over thirty seconds to load.
Q: Every time?
A: Most mornings. Worse at shift change.
Q: Did you report that to Brightloom?
A: Yes. Support tickets and emails. At least nine tickets in
December.
Q: When did Halvorsen terminate the contract?
A: January 15, 2025.
Q: Nothing further.
MS. QUARRIE: No questions.
(Deposition concluded at 2:12 p.m.)
